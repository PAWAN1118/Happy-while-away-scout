Scout’s concept role is to respond to simple simulated events with short human-language phrases. It does not know whether a dog is happy, sad, or anxious from a camera moment.

  • Owner can disable speech and stop the optional camera preview.
  • Scout uses short responses tied to simulated observable events.
  • The physical scent hunt remains the dog’s main activity.
The scent-hunt controls are a simulation. Scout Live can analyze camera snapshots using a pretrained dog detector and simple movement estimates. It cannot reliably identify detailed behaviors or emotions and is not a safety monitor.
